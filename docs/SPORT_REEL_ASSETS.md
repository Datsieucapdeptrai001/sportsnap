# Sport reel backgrounds

These decorative scenes were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the
SportSnap interface. No third-party media was downloaded. The original PNGs were
encoded as WebP for delivery, without changing their composition. These are still
images with CSS pan/zoom and crossfades, not video clips or inference fixtures.
The scenes do not define supported classifier labels; the tag row uses `/api/classes`.

## Assets and generation prompts

### football

Asset: `frontend/public/backgrounds/football.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen decorative website sport background, wide 16:9 landscape composition. Primary request: cinematic action photograph of an adult football player in a plain deep emerald uniform winding up a powerful kick, football suspended above dewy grass, floodlit stadium at night, fine rain and turf particles in the rim light. Wide low-angle shot with the athlete in the right third and the stadium atmosphere spreading across the frame, subject recognizable when center is covered by a translucent web panel, composition also supports center cropping on mobile. Realistic anatomy and correct football equipment. Moody neutral charcoal and emerald grade, restrained bright highlights, subtle natural motion blur in the environment with legible action. No text, no graphics, no logos, no watermarks, no UI.
